CAPCUT LITE
===========

This makes CapCut smaller and cleaner: no AI extras, no extra languages,
no auto-updates, no crash reports, no "Join Pro" pop-ups and no AI cards on the home screen
(the AI-cards part needs Python installed; without it that part is skipped).
It also compresses CapCut on disk, so it takes about half the space.
Editing and exporting work like normal.


SETTING IT UP ON A NEW PC

  1. Install CapCut from capcut.com like you normally would.
     Open it once so it finishes setting up, then close it.

  2. Double-click "Make CapCut Lite.bat".
     A black window opens, does its thing for a minute or two,
     and tells you when it's finished. Press any key to close it.

  3. That's it. Open CapCut with the "CapCut Lite" shortcut on your desktop
     (it hides the AI cards, the EditPilot button and the editor's Generate row, AI avatar tab and the empty-media AI cards and the AI clipper bar and the "AI extend" clip-edge handle and the timeline AI extend and AI edit buttons and the "AI stylize" tab on a selected clip and the "Get 7 days free Pro" button at the top right and the Pro / free-trial and "CapCut Ultra" / "CapCut Pro" offer pop-ups; CapCut's own files stay
     untouched, so online stickers and effects keep working).
     Opening CapCut the normal way just shows normal CapCut.
     The first time you export, set Codec to H.264.


OPTIONAL: TURN OFF CAPCUT'S INTERNET

  "Offline mode ON.bat" stops CapCut from going online,
  so no tracking and no updates. Windows asks for permission, click Yes.

  The only catch: effects and transitions you've never used before
  can't be downloaded while it's on. Use "Offline mode OFF.bat"
  whenever you need new ones.


WHEN CAPCUT UPDATES

  Updates bring the removed stuff back. Just run
  "Make CapCut Lite.bat" again.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  Uninstall CapCut, install it fresh from capcut.com and you have
  normal CapCut again. (CapCut Lite deletes the parts it removes,
  so there is no undo file.)


Keep all these files together. The "_files (don't touch)" folder
is what makes the other files work.

Made for CapCut 9.7.1 (November 2026).
